# Fatigue and breaks

Fatigue slows your reactions and judgement in the same way alcohol does. Late in a shift, small mistakes become more likely.

## Signs you are getting tired

- Cycle times getting slower or less consistent.
- Missing signals, drifting attention, or yawning often.
- More idle time or hesitating between tasks.

## What to do

1. Tell your supervisor if you feel drowsy. It is always OK to stop.
2. Park safely: lower the attachment, engage the hydraulic lockout and parking brake.
3. Take your break outside the cab if you can. Walk, stretch, and drink water.
4. Don't use the end of the shift to "catch up". Rushing when tired is when incidents happen.

## Planning ahead

- Take your scheduled breaks, even when you are behind.
- Get enough sleep before an early shift.
