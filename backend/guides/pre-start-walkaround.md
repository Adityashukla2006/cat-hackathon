# Pre-start walkaround inspection

Do a walkaround at the start of every shift, before you start the engine. Follow your site checklist and the machine's OMM. Report anything unsafe and do not operate until it is cleared.

## Before you walk

- Make sure the machine is parked on level ground with the attachment lowered and the engine off.
- Look under and around the machine for people, obstacles, and fresh fluid puddles.

## Walk the machine in one direction

Work around the machine clockwise so you don't miss a side.

1. **Ground and underside**: fresh puddles of oil, coolant, or fuel point to a leak.
2. **Tracks or tires**: on excavators, look for loose or missing track shoes, damaged rollers, and correct track sag. On wheel loaders, check tires for cuts, bulges, and proper inflation.
3. **Attachment and linkage**: check the bucket, teeth, cutting edge, pins, and retainers for cracks, wear, or missing parts.
4. **Hydraulics**: check hoses and cylinders for leaks, rubbing, cracks, or bulges. Never check for a leak with your hand. Pressurized fluid can pierce skin.
5. **Fluid levels**: check engine oil, coolant, and hydraulic oil at the sight gauges, following the OMM.
6. **Lights, mirrors, cameras, and alarms**: clean them and confirm they work, including the travel alarm.
7. **Steps, handholds, and cab**: clear mud, ice, or oil off steps and handholds. Check the glass, the seatbelt, and the fire extinguisher.

## After the walkaround

- Record what you checked and any defects in your pre-start log.
- If something is damaged or leaking, tag the machine out and tell your supervisor.
