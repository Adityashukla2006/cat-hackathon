# Seatbelt and cab safety

Always follow your site rules and the machine's Operation and Maintenance Manual (OMM). This guide is a summary, not a replacement.

## Why the seatbelt matters

The cab's rollover and falling-object protection (ROPS/FOPS) only protects you if you stay inside it. In a rollover or sudden stop, an unbelted operator can be thrown against the cab or out of the door. The seatbelt keeps you in the protected zone.

## Buckle up before the engine starts

1. Climb in using three points of contact (two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand).
2. Close the door and adjust the seat so you reach the pedals and controls without stretching.
3. Fasten the seatbelt low across your hips, not your stomach. Check it is not twisted.
4. Only then start the engine.

If the tablet shows **Seatbelt: Unbuckled** while the engine is on, stop, lower the attachment to the ground, and buckle up before moving.

## Keep it on

- Keep the seatbelt fastened the whole time the engine is running, including short moves and when waiting in a queue.
- Replace a belt that is frayed, cut, or does not latch. Report it to your supervisor and do not operate until it is fixed.

## Leaving the cab

1. Lower the bucket or attachment fully to the ground.
2. Engage the hydraulic lockout (control lock lever) and the parking brake.
3. Idle down and shut off the engine as your OMM describes.
4. Unbuckle, then climb down facing the machine using three points of contact. Never jump.
