# Emergencies and incident reporting

## In any emergency

1. Stop the machine safely: lower the attachment and engage the hydraulic lockout.
2. Call your supervisor or site emergency number straight away.
3. Follow your site emergency plan.

## Contact with overhead power lines

- Stay in the cab if it is safe to do so and warn others to keep away from the machine.
- Only leave if there is a fire or other immediate danger, and then follow your site's emergency exit procedure for electrical contact. Call the supervisor straight away.

## Fire

- Shut off the engine if you can do it safely, get out, and move away. Raise the alarm.
- Only use the extinguisher on a small fire if you are trained and have a clear escape route.

## Reporting incidents and near misses

- Report every incident and near miss, even if nobody was hurt. Near-miss reports prevent the next accident.
- Use the voice button on the tablet to record what happened, where, and what you did. The report is sent to your supervisor and the location can warn other machines.
- Include: what happened, where, whether anyone was hurt, damage, and what you did straight after.
