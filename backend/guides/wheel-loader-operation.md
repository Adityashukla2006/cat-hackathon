# Wheel loader operation basics

Check the OMM for your machine's controls before operating.

## Travel

- Carry the bucket low, around axle height, when travelling. This keeps the machine stable and your view clear.
- Slow down on turns. Wheel loaders articulate in the middle, so the rear swings out. Keep people clear of the pinch point at the articulation joint.
- Use the travel alarm, mirrors, and camera. Stop if you lose sight of a spotter.

## Working a stockpile

1. Approach the pile square, with the bucket flat on the ground.
2. Drive into the pile at a steady low speed, then raise and curl the bucket to fill it.
3. Back out straight before you turn.
4. Don't undercut a high face. It can collapse onto the machine.

## Visibility

- Stop and check when reversing. Most wheel loader incidents happen while reversing.
- Keep windows, mirrors, and cameras clean.
