# Idle time and fuel efficiency

Idling burns fuel, adds engine hours, and wears components without moving any material.

## Reduce idle time

- If you expect to wait more than a few minutes (a late truck, a survey check, a blocked area), idle down. Shut the engine off if your site rules allow.
- Call your supervisor or dispatcher when you are waiting on something, so work can be reshuffled.
- Plan the next task during natural pauses instead of stopping.

## Operate efficiently

- Use the machine's economy or power mode as your OMM recommends for the task.
- Smooth, combined movements use less fuel than jerky single-function movements.
- Keep the digging and dumping positions close together to cut swing and travel time.
- Keep the bucket teeth and cutting edge in good condition. Worn teeth make every pass harder.
