# Excavator operation basics

This guide covers the common excavator control pattern. Your machine may use a different pattern. Check the control pattern decal in the cab and the OMM before you operate.

## Joystick controls (common ISO pattern)

- **Left joystick**: forward and back moves the **stick** out and in. Left and right **swings** the upper structure.
- **Right joystick**: forward and back lowers and raises the **boom**. Left and right curls the **bucket** in and dumps it out.
- Small, smooth movements give better control than full-lever jerks.

## Digging cycle

1. Position the tracks so you swing as little as possible between the dig and the dump.
2. Lower the boom and extend the stick to start the cut.
3. Pull the stick in while curling the bucket to fill it. Keep the cut steady, don't bounce the bucket.
4. Raise the boom as you curl the bucket in fully.
5. Swing to the dump point, dump, and swing back while lowering for the next pass.

## Trenching

- Dig in layers and keep the trench walls to the profile set out by your supervisor.
- Place spoil well back from the trench edge.
- Never let anyone enter a trench unless it is protected as your site requires.

## Swinging safely

- Check that the swing area is clear before every swing. Watch for people and for the counterweight's tail swing.
- Never swing a load over people or truck cabs.
