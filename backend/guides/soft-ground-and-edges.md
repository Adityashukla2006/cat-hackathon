# Working on soft ground, slopes, and edges

Wet and soft ground is one of the most common causes of machines tipping or getting stuck. Follow your site's ground-condition rules and your supervisor's instructions.

## Warning signs

- Tracks or tires sinking, or the machine leaning more than expected.
- Cracks appearing in the ground near an edge, trench, or ramp.
- Water pooling, fresh fill, or ground that was recently excavated.

## If the machine starts to sink or lean

1. **Stop** travelling and stop digging.
2. Keep the attachment low. Do not raise a loaded bucket.
3. Move back slowly the way you came, onto ground you know is firm, if you can do it safely.
4. If you cannot safely move, stay in the cab with your seatbelt on and call your supervisor.
5. Report the location so it can be marked as a hazard for other machines.

## Edges, ramps, and trenches

- Keep well back from edges, trench walls, and the crest of a ramp. Soft ground near an edge can give way under the machine's weight.
- On excavators, position the tracks at right angles to an edge where possible so the idlers, not the side of the track, face the edge.
- Travel straight up and down slopes, not across them. Keep the bucket low.
- Do not undercut the ground you are standing on.

## After rain

- Expect longer task times and slower cycles. Go slow and do not chase the schedule.
- Re-check areas that were firm yesterday.
